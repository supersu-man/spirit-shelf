# 🍸 Spirit Shelf

Welcome to **Spirit Shelf**, your ultimate digital companion for cocktail enthusiasts! Discover, explore, and manage a vast collection of cocktails and spirits. Whether you're a seasoned mixologist or just looking to try something new, Spirit Shelf has you covered with an intuitive Angular-based web application.

## ✨ Features

- **Browse Cocktails**: Explore a rich database of cocktails with detailed recipes and ingredients.
- **Dashboard**: Get an overview of your favorite drinks and personalized recommendations.
- **Details View**: Dive deep into each cocktail with step-by-step instructions and tips.
- **Home Page**: A welcoming landing page to kickstart your cocktail journey.
- **Responsive Design**: Enjoy a seamless experience on desktop and mobile devices.
